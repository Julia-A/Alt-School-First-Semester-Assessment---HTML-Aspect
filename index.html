My Biography

Hi, my name is Julia. I studied Computer Science at Babcock University, and I’ve always been interested in technology and how things work behind the scenes.

My journey hasn’t been completely straightforward though. At some point, I also had the opportunity to intern in the oil industry, which exposed me to a different kind of work environment and helped me see how technology can fit into industries that are not necessarily considered tech companies.

Over time, I found myself getting more interested in software development, especially the process of building things and solving problems with code. I’ve worked with technologies like Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Supabase, and Vercel, and I’ve had experience working on backend systems, APIs, authentication, databases, and technical documentation.

One thing I really like about technology is that there’s always something new to learn. You can know quite a bit and still come across a problem that forces you to think differently, research, experiment, and improve.

Outside of software development, I’m currently also learning motion graphics. I like the creative side of it and the idea of using visuals and movement to communicate ideas in a more interesting way. I’m still exploring that space, but it’s something I genuinely enjoy learning.

Right now, I’m in the AI-Powered Fullstack Engineering track for the Karatu Cohort 2026. I joined because I want to grow beyond what I already know, become stronger as a fullstack engineer, and understand how AI can be incorporated into the way we build software today. I’m looking forward to learning, building, making mistakes, improving, and seeing just how much I can grow through the program.

Why I Joined AltSchool Africa

I joined AltSchool Africa because I wanted a more structured way to improve my software engineering skills and move beyond learning things randomly on my own. I already have a background in Computer Science and some experience with software development, but I know there is still a lot I need to learn, especially when it comes to building complete, production-ready applications.

What attracted me to AltSchool was the practical approach to learning and the opportunity to actually build while learning. I also liked the idea of learning alongside other people instead of trying to figure everything out alone.

The AI-Powered Fullstack Engineering track stood out to me because AI is becoming a major part of how software is built and used. I want to understand how to properly integrate AI into applications rather than just use AI tools from the outside. This connects directly to my goal of becoming a stronger fullstack engineer who can confidently build useful products from start to finish.

My Goals for the School of Engineering

  • I want to become more confident in fullstack development, especially frontend development, system design, working with databases, APIs, and integrating AI into applications.

  • I want to build a complete application that solves an actual problem for people and not just something that exists only as a portfolio project.

  • I want to get better at working with other developers, communicating my ideas clearly, contributing to team projects, and also becoming more consistent with my learning.

  • I want to be able to confidently work as a fullstack software engineer and take on roles where I can contribute to real products, while also continuing to grow in AI-powered software development.

My Learning Plan

How will I work towards my goals?

I plan to stay consistent with the programme instead of waiting until assessments or deadlines before studying. I want to review what I learn regularly and, more importantly, practise it by actually writing code.

For every major concept I learn, I want to try to build something with it, even if it is just a small feature or mini project. I also plan to use documentation more often, ask questions when I get stuck, and learn from the people in my cohort.

I’ll track my progress mainly through the projects I’m able to complete and how independently I can solve problems over time. I also want to keep my work on GitHub so I can look back and actually see how much my skills have improved.

What would I like to build?

I would like to build an AI-powered fullstack application that solves a real everyday problem. I’m particularly interested in building something where AI is actually useful to the user rather than adding AI just because it is popular. It could help users organise information, make better decisions, automate repetitive tasks, or make a process that is normally stressful much easier.

For me, the most important thing would be building something that people can genuinely use. I’d like the project to challenge me to work on the frontend, backend, database, authentication, APIs, AI integration, and deployment so that by the end of it, I would have gone through the complete process of building a real product.
